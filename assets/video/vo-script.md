# ATV Design Landing Hero — Voiceover Script

**Voice ID:** `af_bella` — warm, confident, slightly playful. Conversational but authoritative. Matches the positioning of "your design agent."

**Target WPM:** 150  
**Target Duration:** 70–75 seconds  
**Word Count:** ~180 words

---

## Script

Your GitHub Copilot subscription. Your laptop. Real design artifacts.

Meet ATV Design—an open-source AI agent that turns your design prompts into HTML, JSX, and slides. No web accounts. No API handshakes. Just Copilot, connected locally via OAuth, generating the prototypes you need.

Imagine: you describe a SaaS landing page hero. You hit generate. And… there it is. A live, interactive prototype. Right on your machine. Tweak the layout. Adjust the colors. Export it as a web component, a React JSX file, or a PDF—all without leaving your workspace.

ATV Design works with Copilot out of the box. Plus Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Ollama if you prefer.

It's local-first. No subscriptions. No tracking. Your designs, your credentials, your rules.

Open source. MIT-licensed. Run it locally today.
